A little about us

We build brands layer by layer.

LumiLayer is a fictional digital studio for this educational project. Our story shows how clear thinking and considered craft can make digital work more useful.

Our story

Strong digital work has more than one layer.

Every project begins with a question: what should this experience help someone do? We start with strategy to understand the goal, add design to make the experience clear, and bring it to life with careful code.

Then we look at growth: how can the work keep serving people over time? These layers—strategy, design, code, and growth—guide how LumiLayer approaches every brief.
